Key Architectural Capabilities

🤖

AI Multimodal Triage

Analyzes uploaded complaint image and text description to infer category, severity, confidence level, and responsible department.

🔍

Duplicate Detection

Automatically compares new complaints against historical records using semantic text similarity, category, and spatial location metrics.

📍

Civic Hotspot Detection

Aggregates cluster complaints at the same road or vicinity to detect infrastructure hotspots and escalate priority to URGENT.

⚖️

Algorithmic Priority Score

Calculates an automated urgency score factoring in AI severity, cluster volume, hazard category, and active hotspot states.

🏢

Municipal Department Dispatch

Routes issues directly to Electrical, Roads, Sanitation, Water Supply, Drainage, Traffic, and Public Works teams.

⏱️

Live Status Tracking

Complete citizen timeline tracking from SUBMITTED → ASSIGNED → IN_PROGRESS → RESOLVED with official notes and timestamps.
